Delete your Parligmo account

For the Parligmo app, operated by Parligmo. Updated October 15, 2026.

You can delete your Parligmo account and the data linked to it at any time. This page explains how, what we delete, and what stays.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Parligmo and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Deletion happens right away, and the app returns to a new, empty anonymous profile. The Delete option appears while you are signed in to an account. If you only used Parligmo without creating an account, there is no account to delete, and the steps in the next section cover your options.

If you cannot open the app

  1. On your phone, open the Parligmo page in the Google Play Store.
  2. Scroll to App support and tap the email address shown there.
  3. Send a message with the subject Delete my Parligmo account, from the email address you used to create your account, so we can confirm the request is yours.
  4. We will reply to confirm, and we will delete your data within 30 days of confirming it is you.

If you never created an account, the app used a random anonymous profile that is not linked to your name or email, so we usually cannot tell which profile is yours. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

What we delete

  • Your account record and the email address you used to sign up.
  • Your credit balance and credit history.
  • Your saved analyses and scans, including the details read from your bet slips and the results.
  • Your subscription status record and usage counters.
  • Earlier anonymous profiles that were merged into your account.

What stays, and for how long

  • Payment records. Google Play processes your payments and keeps its own purchase records under Google’s policies. We do not keep our own copy of your credits or purchases after deletion.
  • Backups. Copies in routine backups are overwritten as the backups roll over, and are not used to restore deleted accounts.

Our service providers keep some data under their own policies. The providers we use are listed in our privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. To cancel, open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, choose Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, select Parligmo, and tap Cancel subscription. Do this before you delete your account.

Questions

For anything about your data, contact us through the App support details on the Parligmo page in Google Play. See also our privacy policy.
