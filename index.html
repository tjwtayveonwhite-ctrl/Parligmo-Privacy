Parligmo Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information the Parligmo app (“Parligmo”, “the app”, “we”) collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The app is operated by Parligmo. You can contact us using the developer contact details shown on the Parligmo page in Google Play.

In short: you give us bet-slip screenshots and the numbers you enter, we send them to our service providers to produce your analysis, and we keep a small amount of account and purchase information so the app works. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Bet slips you submit

When you choose a screenshot or take a photo of a bet slip, the image is uploaded for analysis. We also store the details read from it, such as teams, markets, odds, and stake amounts, and the analysis we produce. A slip image can show more than the bet itself, for example a sportsbook username or balance. Please crop out anything you do not want to share before you submit it.

Information you enter

This includes budget or stake amounts and similar settings you type into the app.

Account and identifiers

The app creates a random anonymous profile when you first open it, so you can use it without an account. If you choose to create an optional account, we also collect your email address. We use these identifiers to keep your credits and purchases attached to you.

Purchases and credits

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, gives us your purchase status, subscription tier, and a receipt reference. We do not receive your payment card details. We keep a record of the credits you have received, spent, and purchased.

Technical information

Like most apps, the app and the services it relies on may receive standard technical information, such as your device model, operating system version, app version, language, and diagnostic data about errors or crashes.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos and media: so you can pick a bet-slip screenshot from your device.
  • Camera: so you can photograph a printed bet slip.

We use these only when you choose to add a slip. You can turn either permission off in your device settings, and you can still use the app features that do not need it.

How we use information

  • To analyze your bet slips and show you the results.
  • To run the app: your credits, subscriptions, purchases, and account.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix errors.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the app, and only what they need to do that work:

  • Supabase hosts our database and server functions, which store your anonymous profile, credits, and related records. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that reads and analyzes the bet slips you submit. Your slip image and its details are sent to Anthropic for this purpose. Anthropic privacy policy.
  • Sports odds data providers supply current odds and game information. We send them the games and markets we need to look up, not your personal information.
  • RevenueCat manages purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Google processes payments and provides Google Play services. Google privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep information, and deleting it

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymize it. Data stored on your device, such as your analysis history and settings, is removed when you uninstall the app.

To ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it, contact us using the details in the Contact section below. We will confirm once the deletion is done. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, such as purchase records.

Age requirement

Parligmo is for people who are 18 or older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the information.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one you live in. When we choose providers, we look for ones that protect information appropriately.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, or to object to some uses of it. To make a request, contact us using the details in the Contact section below. We will respond as the law requires.

Not betting advice

Parligmo provides analysis for information only. It does not guarantee any result and is not financial or betting advice. Only bet what you can afford to lose. If gambling is a problem for you or someone you know, free, confidential help is available. In the U.S., call 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) or visit ncpgambling.org.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app.
